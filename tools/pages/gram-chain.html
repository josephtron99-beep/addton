  2. 02Validators sign

  3. 03Block sealed

Gram Chain & GCC questions.

01What is Gram Chain?

A high-performance Layer 1 blockchain that uses Proof of Authority consensus to produce a new block about every 0.8 seconds.

02What is GCC?

GCC is Gram Chain's native coin. It is used to pay transaction fees and to move value across the network and the AddTON ecosystem.

03What is wGCC?

wGCC is wrapped GCC on BNB Smart Chain. It represents GCC on BNB Smart Chain so it can be held and moved within the BNB ecosystem.

04When will GCC be listed on exchanges?

Exchange listings are coming soon. Dates and venues will be announced on our official Telegram and X channels only.

05What is Proof of Authority?

A consensus model where approved, identifiable validators produce blocks. It allows fast block times and low costs while keeping validators accountable.
