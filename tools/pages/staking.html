FAQ06

Staking questions.

Ask on Telegram ↗
01What is staking?

Staking means committing your crypto to a staking plan so it can earn rewards over time, instead of sitting idle in a wallet.

02Why stake XLM?

XLM is the native coin of the Stellar network and our featured staking coin, with vault APY of up to 250%+. Rewards compound back into your balance.

03Which coins can I stake?

XLM, Rental Wallet Coin and TON today, with 45+ coins supported across the platform. New coins are added in future updates; the full list is in your dashboard.

04How is APY calculated?

APY (annual percentage yield) is the yearly return including compounding. Rates differ by coin and plan and can change; the current rate for each plan is shown before you stake.

05When do I receive rewards?

Rewards are credited on your plan's schedule and appear in your dashboard as they accrue.

06Do I need a Sponsor ID to join?

Yes. Registration requires the Sponsor (referral) ID of an existing AddTON member.
