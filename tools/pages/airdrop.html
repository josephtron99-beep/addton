FAQ07

Airdrop questions.

Ask on Telegram ↗
01Who can join the AddTON Airdrop?

Anyone with an AddTON account. Register with a Sponsor ID if you don't have one yet, so rewards can be credited to you.

02What can I earn?

Each approved task earns a reward. Current reward amounts for social tasks, videos and articles are shown in your dashboard.

03How are tasks checked?

The AddTON team reviews every submission. Rewards are credited to your balance once a task is approved.

04Can I submit more than one video or article?

Yes. Each original video or article is reviewed and rewarded separately.

05Why was my submission rejected?

Common reasons: the content isn't original, the post or video is private or deleted, or the account doesn't match your AddTON account.

Stay safe: AddTON will never ask for your password, private keys or seed phrase to claim an airdrop. Only trust links on this page and in our official channels.
